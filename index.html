Why Remove EXIF Metadata from Your Photos?

Every digital photo captured on a smartphone or digital camera embeds hidden information called EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) data. This includes sensitive details such as your exact GPS coordinates, date, time, device model, and even software versions. When you share images online on blogs, social media, or forums, anyone can potentially extract this information to track your location.
